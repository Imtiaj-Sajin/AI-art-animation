# House of Owls style guide

## Look

- **Medium:** a sketchbook come to life. Navy ink outlines that "boil" (redrawn 8×/second), crayon fills
  with visible hatching, layered watercolor washes, cream paper with grain showing through the pigment.
- **Palette** (`HD.C` in `lib/hand.js`):
  ink `#1b2140` · night `#27355e` · deep `#141a33` · sea `#2f4a78` · paper `#f3ecdd` ·
  lamp `#ffcf6b` · **accent orange `#f47a3a`** (annotations, key words) · rust `#b5452b` (stamps, diagram notes).
- **Light:** most stories happen at night. Lantern and lighthouse light cut soft holes in the darkness;
  the glow layer adds warmth. Lightning is a short double flicker.
- **Annotation layer:** orange hand-drawn circles, underlines, arrows and handwritten notes appear in
  sync with the narration. They act as the narrator's pen.

## Type

- Handwriting: **Caveat** (notes, labels, captions)
- Titles and documents: **IM Fell English** (old-print feel, italic for quotes)
- Brand: **Fredoka** (THE OWL SOCIETY, stamps)

## Motion

- Camera always drifts (never fully static); push in on the detail being named.
- Text writes on left-to-right; shapes draw on along their path.
- Scene transitions: watercolor **bleed** (default), **fade** for emotional beats, **cut** for shocks.
- Hold still moments for weight: the stopped clock, "Nothing answered."

## Sound

- Narrator: Kokoro `am_michael`, en-us, speed 0.92. Studio/podcast processing, **no reverb on the voice**.
- Bed: sea, wind and rain matched to the scene; "muffle" when indoors.
- Score: changes every scene: warm string pad on a new chord, sparse piano on reflective moments,
  a low heartbeat pulse under tension. Sits ~13–15 dB under the voice, swells in the pauses.
- Signature cues: owl **hoot** + **music box** on the title and outro; low **boom** on reveals.

## Brand moments

- **Title card:** dark woods, the owl's eyes glow, it lifts the lantern (light floods in),
  "HOUSE OF OWLS presents" then the title in IM Fell.
- **Outro:** owl on a branch with lantern; "Keep watching. Keep learning."; dozens of eyes open in the
  dark; Owl Society emblem draws itself; "THE OWL SOCIETY". The owl winks on the last line.
