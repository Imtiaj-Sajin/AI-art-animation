## More popular-but-unproven ideas (user preference, 2026-10-29)

The user wants **famous stories with no proof either way** (debated, unexplained). Shorts must be **≤ 1:08**.

- **D.B. Cooper** (1971): hijacker parachuted into a storm with $200,000, never found.
- **The Voynich Manuscript**: a 600-year-old book in an unreadable language.
- **Oak Island Money Pit**: 200+ years of digging, no confirmed treasure.
- **Dyatlov Pass** (1959): tent cut open from the inside (popular; the avalanche explanation is strong but debated).
- **Lake Nyos** (1986): 1,746 dead in one night (explained, CO₂, but great hook).
