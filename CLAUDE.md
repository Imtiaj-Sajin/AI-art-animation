# Context for Claude

This repo is the production studio for **House of Owls**, the user's YouTube channel of hidden,
mysterious and forgotten *true* stories. Videos are hand-drawn-style animations rendered from code.

## What the user cares about (from their brief, 2026-10-28)

- **Premium and artistic.** The look must feel crafted: ink line-boil, crayon texture, watercolor washes,
  paper grain, warm lantern light against night. "Need to be more creative": every scene needs a visual
  idea (a metaphor, a device, a reveal), not just illustration of the narration.
- **A hook in the first seconds.** Open cold on the most intriguing detail of the story, as a question,
  before any title. The viewer must want the answer within ~5 seconds.
- **The ending is the Owl Society, never "subscribe".** The owl mascot appears with its lantern, and the
  narrator says some form of: "Keep watching. Keep learning. And one day... you'll be one of us. A member
  of the Owl Society." Glowing eyes open in the dark around the owl.
- **Everything lives in this repo** (code, skills, research, context) so work can continue from any
  machine. Write new knowledge down here, in `docs/`, or in the episode folder, not just in chat.

## The mascot

Round white owl, big round black glasses, the right brow raised (curious and skeptical), orange beak and
feet, grey wings and shading, holding a lantern. Implemented in `lib/owl.js` (`HD.owl`). The user's
reference image is the channel avatar. Keep it consistent across episodes.

## How things fit together

`script.json` → `tools/tts.py` (Kokoro voice `bm_george`, en-gb, speed 0.9) → `build/timeline.json` with
the start and end of every line and scene → scenes in `scenes-*.js` animate against `k.at('lineId')` →
`tools/sound.py` mixes ambience and effects using the same timeline → `tools/render.mjs` renders frames
and muxes `build/mix.wav`. Changing the script means re-running voice, then sound; the animation follows
automatically because it is keyed to line ids, not absolute seconds.

## Rules

- **Facts first.** Every claim in the narration must trace to `research.md`. Label theories as theories.
  Debunk popular myths rather than repeating them (it's part of the channel's credibility).
- **Check your own frames.** After any visual change, render stills (`npm run stills -- t1,t2 <episode>`)
  and look at them before rendering the full video.
- Respect real people: the 1900 keepers have no known photos, so portraits are anonymous silhouettes.
  Don't invent faces or personal details.
- Large binaries (models, rendered MP4s, WAVs) are git-ignored; they are regenerated by the tools.
