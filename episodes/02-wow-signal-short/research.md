# Episode 02 (Short) — The Wow! Signal (research notes)

Fact-checked 2026-10-29. Every narrated claim traces to a line here. Format: YouTube Short, ≤ 1:08.

## Verified facts

| Fact | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Received **15 August 1977**, 22:16 EST | [1] |
| Telescope | **Big Ear**, Ohio State University Radio Observatory, Delaware, Ohio; used for the Ohio SETI program (searching for signals from extraterrestrial civilisations) | [1], [4] |
| Discovery | Astronomer **Jerry R. Ehman** found it **a few days later** while reviewing the computer printout; he circled it and wrote **"Wow!"** beside it | [1], [2] |
| 6EQUJ5 | Not a code: each character is the signal **intensity** in one 12-second sample. Blank/1/2 ≈ background noise; letters are higher values (A=10 … U≈30). **U ≈ 30 standard deviations above the background noise.** | [1], [2] |
| 72 seconds | Big Ear could observe any point for only **72 seconds** as the Earth turned; the signal's rise and fall matched what a real celestial source would produce over that window | [1] |
| Frequency | ~1420 MHz, the **hydrogen line**, a frequency long proposed (Cocconi & Morrison, 1959) as a natural channel for interstellar communication | [1] |
| Direction | Constellation **Sagittarius**, about 2.5° south of the Chi Sagittarii star group | [1] |
| Never repeated | Searches by Ehman (Big Ear, months after); Robert Gray (META 1987, 1989; Very Large Array 1995, 1996); H. Paul Shuch (Green Bank 1995); Gray & Ellingsen (Tasmania, 1999); Breakthrough Listen (2022). **Never detected again.** | [1] |
| Theories | Comet hydrogen clouds (Paris, 2017): **dismissed by astronomers**. Flaring cold hydrogen cloud hit by a magnetar/stellar burst (Méndez et al., 2024 preprint): **unconfirmed**. Human-made interference: also proposed. **None proven.** | [1], [3], [5] |
| Big Ear's fate | Lease ended; land sold; telescope **dismantled in 1998** for a **housing development and golf course** expansion | [6], [7] |

## Wording choices (precision)

- "far above the noise" instead of "30 times louder" (U is ~30 *standard deviations*, not 30× the power).
- "where scientists expected an alien signal might be" = the hydrogen-line rationale; don't claim aliens.
- The red pen: the famous printout shows the circle and "Wow!" in red ink, so it's shown visually but not narrated.
