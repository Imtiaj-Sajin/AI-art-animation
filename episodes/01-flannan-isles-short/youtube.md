# YouTube Shorts publish kit: Episode 01 (Short)

Upload it from the YouTube app or Studio. Videos that are vertical and 3 minutes or shorter become Shorts
automatically. Post the full episode first, then link the Short to it (step below).

**3 Lighthouse Keepers Vanished. One Left His Coat 🧥**


## Settings

- **Related video:** link it to the full episode ("The Lighthouse Keepers..." long video). This puts a
  tappable link to the full story right on the Short, which is the main way Shorts grow long-form views.
- Audience: No, it's not made for kids. Category: Education.
- Tags (Shorts rely on title, description and hashtags more than tags, but add them anyway):
  `flannan isles, lighthouse keepers vanished, unsolved mysteries, true story, mystery shorts, house of owls`

## Tips

- Post the Short 1–3 days after the long video, or the same day once the long video is public, so the link works.
- The first 2 seconds ("THREE MEN VANISHED.") are the hook. Don't add an intro before it.
